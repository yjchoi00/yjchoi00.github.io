I am a Data Science undergraduate at **University College London (UCL)**, where I achieved First Class in Year 1. I am currently an undergraduate AI researcher at **Sogang University's Network Laboratory**, working on game-theoretic modelling of strategic interaction — incentive design and equilibrium analysis.

My interests sit at the intersection of **machine learning, multi-agent systems, and quantitative finance**: agent-based modelling of markets, game theory applied to interacting participants, ML-driven trading strategies, and LLM agents for research and decision-making.

Before university, I served as a Sergeant in the Republic of Korea Army, maintaining electro-optical surveillance systems. I have also worked as a Summer Law Clerk at **Shin & Kim LLC**, researching comparative AI regulation (EU AI Act, U.S. frameworks, Korea's AI Basic Act) and AI sovereignty policy.
